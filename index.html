01 — About

A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate with a habit of turning ideas into working software — whether that's a sleek app, a clever automation, or a script that quietly saves someone an afternoon of manual work.

When I'm not at the keyboard, I'm usually somewhere with an engine or a lens nearby. Cars and motorcycles taught me to care about precision and design that holds up under pressure; photography taught me patience and the value of a good composition — both have a way of sneaking into how I approach code.

I'm always exploring new tech and tinkering with side projects. This page collects a few of them — more get added as I build.
